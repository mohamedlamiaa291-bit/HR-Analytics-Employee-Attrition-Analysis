# 📊 HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Intelligence

A comprehensive project analyzing employee data, studying workforce turnover patterns (Attrition), and evaluating organizational factors to understand why employees leave the company. The project utilizes **Excel** and **Power BI** for data processing and building an interactive executive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective & Context

Employee retention is a critical challenge for modern organizations. This project analyzes a dataset containing **1,470 employee records** to uncover actionable insights into the drivers of voluntary turnover, highlighting an overall attrition rate of **16.1%**.

---

## ❓ Research & Analysis Questions

To fulfill the project objectives, this analysis addresses the following key questions:
1. **Attrition Drivers:** Which salary brackets, age groups, and job roles exhibit the highest turnover rates within the organization?
2. **Compensation Impact:** How strongly does monthly income correlate with employee retention and overall job satisfaction?
3. **Departmental Trends:** Which departments experience the greatest operational strain and employee exits?
4. **Tenure & Promotions:** How do promotion schedules and years at service influence resignation behavior?

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

* **Overall Attrition:** The organizational departure rate stands at **16.1%** (237 employees out of 1,470).
* **Compensation Factor:** Employees in lower salary brackets represent the overwhelming majority of exits, demonstrating a strong link between compensation and retention.
* **Age & Tenure:** The 26 to 35 age demographic registers peak departures, particularly among personnel with fewer years of service.
* **Operational Roles:** Operational positions such as *Laboratory Technicians* and *Sales Representatives* report the highest rates of movement and resignation.
